The correct statement is:

**Biodiversity is part of the nature that is alive.**

Why:
- **Biodiversity** = the variety of living things (genes, species, ecosystems).
- **Nature** is broader: it includes all living organisms **plus** non-living elements like landscapes, water, air, and geological features.

So:
- ❌ Nature is **not** a subset of biodiversity — it’s the other way around.
- ❌ They are **not** the same and cannot be used interchangeably.
- ❌ They are **not** mutually exclusive — biodiversity is contained within nature.
- ✅ Biodiversity represents the **living component** of nature.
