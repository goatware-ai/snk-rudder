Only this statement is true:

**Biodiversity is part of the nature that is alive.**

Why:
- **Nature** is broader; it can include both living and non-living components (or at least the natural world more broadly).
- **Biodiversity** specifically refers to the variety of life/living organisms.
- Therefore, nature is not a subset of biodiversity, they are not interchangeable, and they are not mutually exclusive.
