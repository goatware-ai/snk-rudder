The degree of financial leverage (DFL) formula when preferred stock exists is:

\[
DFL = \frac{EBIT}{EBIT - I - \frac{D_p}{1-T}}
\]

Where:
- EBIT = 375,000
- Interest (I) = 75,000
- Preferred dividends (D_p) = 6,000
- Tax rate (T) = 0.40

First, gross up the preferred dividends to a pre-tax equivalent:

\[
\frac{D_p}{1-T} = \frac{6,000}{0.60} = 10,000
\]

Now compute the denominator:

\[
EBIT - I - \frac{D_p}{1-T} = 375,000 - 75,000 - 10,000 = 290,000
\]

Then:

\[
DFL = \frac{375,000}{290,000} \approx 1.293 \approx 1.29
\]

So the correct answer is **1.29**.
