Rudder Helper

Fills the comparison form from a task's payload.json. It writes only what the payload holds.

One section at a time

Scan first. It reports the task UID and which pane the screen calls Response A. A/B Adjust reads those panes and turns the payload the right way round for this render; a fill is refused while the payload is for another task or written the other way round.
